# Flourishing Futures — Design Decisions

## Typography

- **Primary font**: `Newsreader` — Korean serif typeface used for headings, hero text, vision statements, and the logo
- **Body font**: System sans-serif stack (`-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'Segoe UI', Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`) for body copy, labels, navigation, and UI elements
- **Heading style**: Large, elegant serif with generous line-height. No bold weight — the typeface carries its own presence
- **Navigation**: Uppercase, spaced lettering, sans-serif

## Colour Palette

| Role             | Colour                  | Usage                                                   |
|------------------|-------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------|
| Yellow           | `#FFE500` (bright)      | Top header / logo area background                       |
| Pink / Magenta   | Hot pink gradient       | Hero section background, gradient from pink to white    |
| Blue             | `#0066FF` (bright)      | Accent sections, feature blocks, dark-content panels    |
| White            | `#FFFFFF`               | Main content background                                 |
| Black            | `#000000` / `#111111`   | Primary text, nav text, borders                         |
| Light grey       | `#F5F5F5`              | Subtle background for muted cards/sections              |

- Bold, playful colour use — not corporate/muted
- Colour blocks are used as full-width section backgrounds, not as subtle accents
- Gradients: pink-to-white vertical gradient in hero area

## Logo Treatment

- Typographic logo (no icon/graphic mark)
- Layout: "Flourishing" (small, above) → "AI for Human Possibilities" (main, larger) → "Futures" (small, below, rotated/inverted)
- Centered at top of page
- Sits within the yellow background zone

## Navigation

- **Labels**: `ABOUT` · `LAB` · `RESOURCE HUB` · `COMMUNITY`
- Horizontal, centered below the hero
- Uppercase, sans-serif, spaced
- Active state: coloured text (pink/magenta)
- Sticky or visible bar with thin border

## Layout Principles

- **Centred composition**: Hero text, vision statement, and key content centred on the page
- **Full-width colour blocks**: Sections use bold background colours spanning the full viewport width
- **Two-column body text**: Prose content uses a two-column layout within coloured section blocks
- **Generous whitespace**: Large padding around vision statements and hero text
- **Playful elements**: Smiley face / emoji-style elements in the hero (eyes + smile on pink gradient)

## Hero Section

- Yellow background at top (logo area) transitions to pink/magenta gradient
- Hero question: Large serif text (e.g. "can AI make citizenship more kind?") with selective underlines on key words
- Tone: Provocative, warm, human — not corporate

## Vision Statement

- Full-width white section
- Small label above: programme attribution line
- Large centred serif text: the vision/mission statement
- Generous vertical padding

## Content Sections

- Dark-background sections (blue) for narrative/explanatory content
- White card or panel inset within the dark section for body text
- Two-column layout for longer prose within these panels

## Tone & Feel

- **Playful and opinionated**: Bold colours and friendly elements (smiley faces) balanced with substantive, thoughtful content
- **Not corporate**: Avoids the grey/navy government look — deliberately colourful and human
- **Warm**: The pink/yellow palette conveys warmth, possibility, optimism
- **Accessible**: High contrast text, clear hierarchy, readable at all sizes
- **Micor-interaction**: Use fun micro-interactions to make it delightful

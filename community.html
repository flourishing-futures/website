Conscious Builders Community

Building better products, together

The Conscious Builders Community is a programme developed to bring AI product developers, designers, researchers and ethicists together to build better products. Here, we share, debate, workshop and design.

Through inspiration sessions and hands-on product garages: we help builders to engage in critical conversations, identify potential risks, imagine better possibilities, and stay inspired and connected to the human impact of working with AI.

Work with us

This work requires good minds and hands. You can work with us on...

1

AI Accountability and Evaluation

If you're a business owner, policy leader, product owner, data analyst, or design researcher — we want to work with you on defining metrics and success-factors for AI evaluation, and tracking human impact across product deployments.

2

Platform and Product Design

If you're a product owner, platform developer, or designer — we want to co-design hero platforms and products with human-possibility as a feature, and develop AI harnesses with human-centric principles baked in.

3

Practices, Culture and Change

If you're a practice or team leader — we want to work with you on playbooks for human-centric AI development, upskilling programmes, and forming events, talks and gatherings that go beyond 'how to use' to why it matters.

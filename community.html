Conscious Builders Community

Building better products, together

Conscious Builders Community is a programme developed to bring AI product developers, designers, researchers and ethicists together to build better products.

The programme involves sharing, discussions, hands-on workshops and product design 'clinics' — all encouraging builders to engage in critical conversations, identify potential risks, imagine better possibilities, and stay inspired and connected to the human impact of working with AI.

Whether you're a product owner, designer, engineer, or researcher — if you build AI-powered products and care about their impact on people, this community is for you.

Work with us

This work requires good minds and hands. You can work with us on...

AI Accountability and Evaluation

If you're a business owner, policy leader, product owner, data analyst, or design researcher — we want to work with you on defining metrics and success-factors for AI evaluation, baking AI and human outcome tracking into product development, and tracking human impact across internal and external product deployments.

Platform and Product Design

If you're a product owner, platform developer, product designer, or engineer — we want to work with you on co-designing hero platforms and products with human-possibility as a feature (not an afterthought), and developing AI harnesses and foundation technology with human-centric principles baked in.

Human-AI Practices, Culture and Change

If you're a practice or team leader — we want to work with you on playbooks and principles for human-centric AI development, upskilling programmes for teams on AI literacy that go beyond 'how to use' to when, why and what matters next, and forming events, talks and gatherings.

18 Jun 2026

Human-AI Flourishing Workshop: From Framework to Practice

A hands-on workshop translating the Human Flourishing framework into practical design and evaluation methods for AI teams. Participants explored how the spectrum from Responsible AI to AI for Human Possibility applies to concrete product decisions, and worked through design patterns that preserve human agency in AI-assisted workflows.


Format: Workshop  ·  Audience: AI product teams, designers, policymakers


Recap: Over 40 participants from across GovTech and partner agencies gathered for a half-day workshop focused on bridging the gap between the Flourishing Futures framework and day-to-day AI product decisions. Teams worked through three exercises: mapping their own products against the AI spectrum (from Responsible AI to Flourishing Futures), identifying where human capability is being strengthened or weakened in current workflows, and prototyping design interventions using productive friction patterns. Key takeaways included the importance of measuring human outcomes alongside operational metrics, and the value of "pause points" in AI-assisted workflows where officers are prompted to exercise independent judgement.

21 May 2026

Human-Centred AI: Intentions, Decisions, and Impact

A seminar exploring how design intent shapes AI outcomes — and what it means to build AI systems that serve human agency rather than substitute for it. Drawing from MIT Media Lab research and the emerging Flourishing Futures framework.


Format: Seminar  ·  Audience: Researchers, public officers, AI practitioners


Recap: This inaugural seminar brought together researchers from MIT Media Lab's Cyborg Psychology group, NTU's SWEET Lab, and senior GovTech practitioners for a wide-ranging conversation on the difference between AI that helps people work faster and AI that helps people work better. Speakers presented emerging research on cognitive offloading, the effects of AI confidence signals on human decision-making, and early findings from the SG ImpactBench pilot. The discussion surfaced a critical insight: most AI evaluation today measures task completion, but the effects that matter most — on judgement, learning, and professional growth — operate on longer timescales and require fundamentally different measurement approaches.
